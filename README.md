# Marketing Bot

A collection of prototype versions documenting the evolution of a Telegram-based marketing and business automation bot.

This repository preserves the project's early development history through eight major iterations.

> **Note:** These are historical prototypes, not production releases.

---

## About

The purpose of this repository is to document how the project evolved over time. Each version represents a significant milestone, introducing new ideas, features, and architectural improvements.

Together, these prototypes show the progression from a simple Telegram bot to a much more capable business automation system.

---

## Prototype Timeline

### V1
Initial proof of concept.

### V2
Improved user interaction and conversation flow.

### V3
Expanded business logic and database integration.

### V4
Marketplace-oriented improvements.

### V5
Enhanced order and business management.

### V6
Major feature expansion and backend improvements.

### V7
Structural refinements and stability improvements.

### V8
Final prototype in this prototype series.
